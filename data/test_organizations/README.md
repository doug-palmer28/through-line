# Test Organizations

Ten **fictional** small nonprofits in and around New Haven, CT, for testing the Logic Model Studio. Upload a PDF on the Share step. The neighborhoods and towns are real; the organizations, people, numbers, and stories are invented (synthetic data only).

Each profile has: a fact sheet (status, founded, location, who they serve, budget, staff, volunteers, reach), mission, story, who they serve, 3 programs, funding mix, what they track today, what they're wrestling with, and a short anonymized story. None includes a logic model.
